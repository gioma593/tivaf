✨ diagnostic toolkit for wine regions

TIVAF

Tourism‑Institutional Value Appropriation Framework

A practical, low‑cost diagnostic to understand if wine tourism really benefits small, family‑run wineries — especially where data is scarce.

📖 How it works → 🔍 The four pillars

🧭 The problem we solve

🍇

In many wine regions (especially with small family wineries):

  • 📉 Official stats don’t separate tourism sales from others
  • 📊 Commercial databases (Orbis, etc.) miss small producers
  • 🤝 Wineries rarely share detailed financial data

Result: Consortiums and policymakers lack evidence to know if wine tourism creates real local value.

🩺

TIVAF = a health check‑up for a wine region

Just like a doctor measures blood pressure, heart rate and temperature, TIVAF measures four vital signs. No need for perfect data — it’s built for data‑poor, small‑firm environments.

“A transparent, replicable diagnostic — not a magic bullet, but a first practical step.”

🏛️ The four pillars · TIVAF vital signs

01
Institutional thickness audit
Do wineries act like a team or like isolated players? We measure shared infrastructure, consortium storytelling, inter‑winery signaling and collective branding.
🔍 Example: Presence of DOQ/DOCG plaques, wine‑route signage, staff mentioning appellation rules, brochures of other local wineries.
📊 Output: Institutional Thickness Index (1–5)
02
Multi‑channel price mapping
Who captures the value? We compare the same entry‑level wine across three channels: supermarket (GDO), specialist wine shop, and direct‑to‑consumer (winery door).
🛒 → 🍷 → 🏠
“Commodity baseline” → “Information premium” → “Tourism premium”
💰 Output: price gaps & channel value capture
03
Digital terroir & narrative scoring
Do wineries sell a bottle or a story? We analyse website + social media (Instagram/Facebook) with a 4‑indicator score: narrative focus, information density, engagement depth, experiential realm.
📱 “Rich territorial storytelling vs. pure price/promotion — and how visitors interact online.”
📈 Output: Digital Identity Score (1–5)
04
Hedonic pricing under data opacity
A simple statistical model that relates price to tourism status, institutional thickness, digital identity and basic product attributes. No huge dataset needed — it’s exploratory and diagnostic.
📐 “Does tourism actually correlate with higher prices, after accounting for region and quality?”
⚙️ Output: exploratory associations, not causation — but actionable for governance.

🌍 How TIVAF works in practice

🇮🇹🍇

Pignoletto DOCG
Colli Bolognesi, Italy

🇪🇸🍷

Priorat DOQ
Catalonia, Spain

20 wineries (10 per region) – half with active wine tourism, half without.
For each winery we:

  • ✅ Conducted in‑situ institutional observation (Pillar 1)
  • ✅ Recorded prices in supermarkets, specialist shops and at the winery door (Pillar 2)
  • ✅ Coded digital presence (website + social media) with double coder reliability (Cohen’s kappa >0.60) (Pillar 3)
  • ✅ Estimated a parsimonious hedonic model (Pillar 4)

Initial insight: Tourism‑integrated wineries tend to command higher direct‑to‑consumer prices, richer territorial narratives and stronger institutional linkages – especially in the more institutionally “thick” Priorat region.

⚖️ Why TIVAF is different

Conventional approachTIVAF approach
Requires large datasets (hundreds of wineries)✅ Works with small samples (≈20 wineries)
Needs detailed financial reports / firm‑level accounts✅ Uses publicly observable prices and physical/digital signs
Treats institutions as background context✅ Measures institutional thickness directly with rubric
Ignores online storytelling & digital terroir✅ Quantifies digital narratives and engagement (Digital Identity Score)
Aims for generalizable causal estimates (often infeasible for small wineries)✅ Provides actionable diagnostics for governance and strategy

📂 Ready‑to‑use templates

TIVAF includes field‑tested observation forms, price mapping sheets, digital scoring rubrics, and a codebook with anchor descriptions. You don’t need a PhD in economics to apply it.

  • Institutional‑thickness audit form (9 indicators, 1–5 Likert)
  • Multi‑channel price mapping form (GDO, specialist, DTC + strategic bypass coding)
  • Digital Identity scoring sheet (narrative focus, information density, engagement depth, experiential realm)
  • Inter‑rater reliability protocol (Cohen’s kappa) for robust diagnostics

🔎 What TIVAF does (and does not do)

✅ Provides diagnostic & exploratory evidence
Generates hypotheses and actionable insights for governance.
✅ Works where conventional econometrics fails
Ideal for small‑N, data‑poor environments (small wineries, emerging GI regions).
⚠️ Not a causal treatment‑effect estimator
Results are correlations within an analytic narrative — but strong enough to inform policy.
