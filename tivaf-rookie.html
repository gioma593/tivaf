TIVAF: the wine region health check‑up

Tourism‑Institutional Value Appropriation Framework – a simple toolkit to see if wine tourism is actually helping small family wineries.

🩺🍷

Think of TIVAF like a medical check‑up for a wine region. A doctor doesn’t guess – they measure your heart rate, blood pressure, and temperature. TIVAF measures four “vital signs” of a wine tourism area. No need for expensive data or secret numbers.

🤔 The problem: we don’t know what’s working

Many beautiful wine regions – especially those with small, family‑run wineries – have a frustrating problem:

  • 📉 Official statistics don’t separate tourism sales from other sales.
  • 📊 Big commercial databases (like Orbis) often miss small wineries completely.
  • 🤝 Wineries don’t share their private financial numbers.

So when a wine consortium or a tourism board asks: “Is our wine tourism bringing real money to local families?” – they usually cannot get a clear answer. TIVAF was built to solve exactly that.

🏛️ The four vital signs (the pillars)

01
Teamwork check

Do wineries cooperate? Do they share signs, promote each other, and talk about their consortium?

🔍 We look for: Wine route logos, staff mentioning appellation rules, brochures of other local wineries in the tasting room.
02
Price detective

Where does the money go? We compare the same bottle of wine in three places: supermarket, specialist wine shop, and directly at the winery.

🛒 → 🍷 → 🏠
“Lowest price” → “Higher price (info premium)” → “Highest price (tourism premium)”
03
Digital storytelling

Do they sell a bottle or a story? We check websites and social media: do they talk about the land, the history, the experience – or just prices?

📱 We score: narrative focus, info density, engagement (comments vs likes), and how they describe the visit.
04
The price & tourism link

A simple math check: after accounting for wine quality, do wineries with tourism, good teamwork, and great online stories have higher prices?

📐 “We don’t claim to prove cause – but we show clear patterns that help decisions.”

🌍 Real example: Italy vs Spain

We tested TIVAF in two wine regions:

  • Pignoletto DOCG (Colli Bolognesi, Italy)
  • Priorat DOQ (Catalonia, Spain)

We visited 20 wineries (10 per region). Half had active wine tourism, half didn’t. We used simple forms to observe, record prices, and score their websites. The result? Wineries with strong teamwork and rich digital stories tended to charge higher prices at their cellar door – especially in Priorat, where the consortium is very active.

📊 How TIVAF is different from old‑school methods

Old wayTIVAF way (the rookie‑friendly way)
Needs hundreds of wineries✅ Works with just 20 wineries
Requires secret financial reports✅ Uses public prices and what you can see/read online
Ignores cooperation and storytelling✅ Measures teamwork and digital stories directly
Aims for perfect “proof” (often impossible)✅ Gives actionable clues for better decisions

👥 Who can use TIVAF?

🍇 Wine consortiums 🗺️ Tourism offices & wine routes 🏛️ Regional policymakers 🎓 Researchers (but also students!)

The best part? TIVAF comes with ready‑to‑use forms – you don’t need a PhD to pick it up.

🍾💡

The bottom line:

TIVAF helps small wine regions answer one powerful question: “When a tourist visits a winery, how much of that value stays with the local family – and how can we measure it without expensive data?”

It’s not magic. It’s a transparent, low‑cost health check‑up for your wine territory.
